# AI vocabulary and phrase reference

Words and phrases that appear at far higher rates in AI-generated text than in human writing. Use density as the signal: one hit means little, several per paragraph is a tell. When replacing, prefer the plain word, or delete the word and keep the claim.

## Promotional phrases

Cut or replace with plain description: nestled in, in the heart of, breathtaking, stunning, must-visit, renowned, world-class, unparalleled, rich cultural heritage, natural beauty, commitment to excellence, harness the power of, embark on a journey.

## Significance phrases

Cut; state the fact: marking a pivotal moment, is a testament to, serves as a reminder, underscores the importance, reflects broader trends, setting the stage for, key turning point, evolving landscape, indelible mark, shaping the future of, plays a crucial role, deeply rooted.

## Throat-clearing and emphasis crutches

Cut and state the point: Here's the thing:, Here's why X, The uncomfortable truth is, It turns out, Let me be clear, The truth is, I'll say it again, I'm going to be honest, Can we talk about, Full stop., Period., Let that sink in., Make no mistake, This matters because, Here's why that matters, What if I told you, Here's what I mean:, Think about it:, Plot twist:, Spoiler:, You already know this, but.

## Chatbot phrases

Delete on sight in prose: I hope this helps!, Let me know if you'd like..., Would you like me to..., Feel free to..., Don't hesitate to..., Happy to help!, Here is an overview/summary/breakdown, Of course!, Certainly!, Absolutely!, I'd be happy to..., Is there anything else..., Great question!, Excellent point!, You're absolutely right!, without further ado, let's dive in.

## Hedging stacks

Collapse to one qualifier or none: could potentially, might possibly, may potentially, perhaps potentially, it could be argued that, one could argue, it is possible that, I'm confident that.

## Generic conclusions

Cut; end on the last concrete fact: the future looks bright, exciting times lie ahead, continue this journey, journey toward excellence, step in the right direction, only time will tell, the possibilities are endless, poised for growth, watch this space.

## Empty intensifiers and hedge adverbs

Cut when they add no meaning: really, very, actually, truly, genuinely, literally, deeply, fundamentally, inherently, inevitably, incredibly, importantly, crucially, interestingly, simply, just, honestly.

Keep adverbs that carry information ("fails intermittently," "legally required"). The tell is empty emphasis, not the part of speech.
